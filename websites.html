Why this process works

The order isn't arbitrary. Each step sits where it does for a reason.

Why define the goal before designing?

Because a beautiful website can still fail if nobody knows what it's supposed to accomplish.

Why understand the audience before writing the pages?

Because the website isn't written for the business owner. It's written for the people the business wants to reach.

Why structure before visual design?

Because visual design should help people navigate and understand information, not hide a poorly structured website.

Why test after development?

Because a website that looks right on the designer's screen isn't necessarily a website that works well for everyone.
