What makes a good employee?

Having one does not make them useful. These are the basics every website has to get right before it can do any real job.

Works well

Fast

Visitors leave slow pages before reading a word. Speed is your first impression.

Reliable

It is there when people need it, with no broken pages or dead forms.

Mobile-friendly

Many visitors arrive on a phone, so it has to work properly on one.

Accessible

Everyone can use it, including people with disabilities, older devices or slow connections.

Easy to use

Easy to understand

A visitor should know what you do and what to do next within seconds.

Easy to navigate

People find what they came for without hunting.

Well-designed

Design that guides attention and builds confidence, not decoration.

Gets found and proven

Discoverable

People searching for what you offer can actually find you.

Measurable

You can see what it is doing, so you can improve it.

A website can have beautiful visuals and still be a terrible business asset.
