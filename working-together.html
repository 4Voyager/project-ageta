A few things worth knowing

Projects don’t always run perfectly smoothly. Here is how the common situations are handled.

What if I don’t like the design?

You review the design at its checkpoint and tell me what isn’t working. I refine it through the agreed revision rounds.

What if I need something that wasn’t included?

I explain the change and price it before doing any work. Nothing is added to your project without your approval.

What if I’m late providing content?

The timeline moves with it. I tell you how a delay affects the launch date, so there are no surprises.

What if the project needs to pause?

That is fine. We agree what is finished, what comes next and how to pick things up again when you are ready.

What if I change my mind halfway through?

Changes of direction are normal. I explain what they mean for scope and payment, and you decide how to proceed.

What if I don’t have all the content yet?

We start with what you have. I structure the website so content can be added as it becomes ready, and I show you what is needed and when.
