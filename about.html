Who I am

I'm Samwel, a web designer and developer based in Kenya. I design, build and look after websites for businesses and individuals who want theirs to do real work.

I got into this the way many people do, through curiosity. First about how websites are made, then about why some of them work so much better than others. The second question is the one that stuck, and it is why I care as much about what a website is for as how it looks.

I enjoy the projects where the thinking matters as much as the building: working out what a business needs, then making a website that delivers it.

Why I build websites this way

A website is easy to build. A useful website is harder.

I'm interested in the places where design, technology, communication, psychology, business and user experience meet. That is why I don't start a project by asking how many pages you want.

My approach

Five principles that shape every project.

Purpose before pages

Every website should have a reason for existing.

Clarity before decoration

Design should help people understand, not simply look impressive.

People before technology

The technology should serve the visitor and the business.

Evidence before assumptions

Research and observation should inform important decisions.

Honesty over selling

If a website isn't the right solution for a problem, I'll say so. Should I have a website?
